Privacy

Last updated October 2026. Short version: we collect as little as we can.

No cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no third-party trackers or ad scripts.

Page-view counts

We count page views without cookies or identifiers. Each count records the page path, the referring site’s domain and any campaign tag in the link. Nothing ties a view to you.

Email

We store your email address only if you sign up for the weekly email. We use it to send that email and Season Pass announcements. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Payments

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and processes your payment. We receive your email address, order details and license key from them, never your card details.

Your browser

If you activate a Season Pass, your license key is saved in your browser’s local storage so Pro stays on. Remove it any time from the Season Pass page.
