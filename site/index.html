        Metric glossary
        What are expected fantasy points (xFP)?

        Every target, carry and pass attempt is valued at what similar plays (same depth, same field position, same down) score on average across the league this season. Add them up and you get the points a player's opportunities are worth, regardless of how they actually turned out.

        What does points over expected (FPOE) tell me?

        Actual PPR points minus xFP. A big positive number means a player is converting his chances, or running hot on touchdowns. Read it next to efficiency (EPA, success rate, separation) to tell skill from luck.

        What is the Fieldglass Score?

        A 0–100 rating within each position: 50% opportunity (xFP volume), 20% efficiency (points over expected, regressed for sample size), 15% usage trend and 15% rest-of-season schedule. The player card shows each part so you can see why.

        Where does the data come from?

        Play-by-play, weekly stats, snap counts, Next Gen Stats and advanced stats from nflverse, an open community data project (CC-BY 4.0). We refresh overnight during the season.
