About

How it works

Fieldglass is a small, independent fantasy football analytics tool. It turns public weekly player data into the handful of numbers that help you set a lineup or plan for the fantasy playoffs.

The numbers

  • Expected fantasy points: every target, carry and pass attempt valued at what similar plays score on average this season.
  • Points over expected: actual PPR points minus expected, the part of production that came from the player rather than the volume.
  • Efficiency: EPA, success rate, Next Gen Stats and charting-free advanced stats, ranked as percentiles within each position.
  • Team environment: neutral pass rate over expected, pace, plays and red-zone trips per game.
  • Schedule and matchups: points and EPA each defense allows to each position, shrunk toward league average early in the season.
  • The Fieldglass Score: 50% opportunity, 20% efficiency, 15% trend, 15% schedule, 0–100 within the position.

Every metric is defined in the metric glossary.
