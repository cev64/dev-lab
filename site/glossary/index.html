Metric glossary

Every number on the site in a sentence or two. Free metrics are available to everyone; Season Pass metrics are shown for every player with a pass.

The scoreUsageOpportunityEfficiencyTeam environmentDefenses and schedule

The score

Fieldglass ScoreFree
A 0–100 rating within each position that blends opportunity (50%), efficiency (20%), usage trend (15%) and rest-of-season schedule (15%). It ranks players the way a manager should: volume first, then what they do with it.
Opportunity sub-scoreSeason Pass
Percentile within the position of expected fantasy points per game, discounted slightly for players with very few games so a one-week spot starter cannot top the list.
Efficiency sub-scoreSeason Pass
Percentile within the position of points over expected per game, regressed toward zero for small samples (opportunities divided by opportunities plus 40).
Trend sub-scoreSeason Pass
50 plus 50 times the usage trend, so a player whose role is growing scores above 50.
Schedule sub-scoreSeason Pass
Rest-of-season schedule ease for the player’s position (50 when no games remain).

Usage

PPGFree
PPR fantasy points per game played, season to date. L3 is the same over the last three games played.
Snap shareFree
Share of his team’s offensive snaps the player was on the field for, in the games he played.
Target shareFree
Player targets divided by team targets in the same games.
Air-yards shareFree
Share of the team’s intended air yards that went to the player.
WOPRFree
Weighted opportunity rating: 1.5 × target share + 0.7 × air-yards share. The best single number for receiver opportunity.
Rush shareFree
Player carries divided by team carries in the same games.
Touches per gameFree
Carries plus receptions per game.
Targets per snapFree
Targets divided by offensive snaps. A proxy for how often a receiver is the read when he is on the field.
Usage trendFree
Last three games against the season for the position’s key opportunity measure (WOPR for WR and TE, rush plus target share for RB, points per game for QB), from −100% to +100%.

Opportunity

Expected fantasy points (xFP)Free
Each target, carry and pass attempt is valued at the league-average PPR outcome of similar plays this season (by air yards and field position for targets and passes, by field position and down for carries). xFP per game is what a player’s opportunities are worth.
xFP rankFree
Rank of xFP per game within the position. 1 is the most opportunity in the league.
aDOTFree
Average depth of target: air yards divided by targets. A style measure, not a quality one: deep threats run high, slot and backfield receivers low.
End-zone targetsFree
Targets thrown at or beyond the goal line.
Red-zone target shareFree
Share of the team’s targets inside the opponent’s 20 that went to the player.
Red-zone carry shareFree
Share of the team’s carries inside the 20.
Inside-10 carry shareFree
Share of the team’s carries inside the 10, where most rushing touchdowns happen.
High-value touches (HVT)Free
Targets plus carries inside the 10, per game. These touches are worth several times an ordinary carry.
Third-down and two-minute target shareFree
For running backs: share of team targets on 3rd and 4th down or with two minutes or less left in a half. Marks the passing-down back.

Efficiency

Points over expected (FPOE)Season Pass
Actual PPR points per game minus xFP per game. Positive means the player out-produced his opportunities; touchdowns drive most of the swing, so expect some of it to regress.
EPASeason Pass
Expected points added: how much a play changed the offense’s expected points, given down, distance and field position. Shown per target, per rush and per dropback.
Success rateSeason Pass
Share of plays with positive EPA. Steadier than EPA because one long play cannot dominate it.
CPOESeason Pass
Completion percentage over expected for a quarterback, in percentage points, after accounting for throw difficulty.
YAC over expectedSeason Pass
Yards after the catch above what is expected from the catch point and defender positions (Next Gen Stats), per reception.
SeparationSeason Pass
Average yards between the receiver and the nearest defender when the ball arrives (Next Gen Stats).
CushionSeason Pass
Average yards between the receiver and the nearest defender at the snap. A style measure.
Rush yards over expected (RYOE)Season Pass
Rushing yards per carry above what blocking and defender positions predicted (Next Gen Stats). Rush over expected % is the share of carries that beat expectation.
Yards before and after contactSeason Pass
Rushing yards per carry before first contact (mostly blocking) and after it (mostly the runner).
Broken tacklesSeason Pass
Tackles broken on runs and receptions, season total.
Drop rateSeason Pass
Drops divided by targets. Lower is better.
Pressure rateSeason Pass
Share of a quarterback’s dropbacks under pressure. Lower is better for fantasy output.
Time to throw and aggressivenessSeason Pass
Average seconds from snap to throw, and the share of throws into tight windows. Style measures, shown without a better or worse direction.
PercentileSeason Pass
Where a player ranks within his position, 0 to 100. Lower-is-better metrics are flipped so 100 is always the good end. Efficiency percentiles need at least 15 opportunities.

Team environment

Neutral situationFree
Plays with win probability between 20% and 80% in quarters 1 to 3, when the game script is not forcing a team’s hand.
Pass rateFree
Share of neutral-situation plays that were dropbacks.
PROEFree
Pass rate over expected: neutral dropback rate minus the rate expected from down, distance and field position. Positive means a team chooses to pass.
PaceFree
Average seconds between neutral-situation snaps on the same drive. Fewer seconds means more plays and more fantasy chances.
Plays per gameFree
Offensive pass and run plays per game.
Red-zone trips per gameFree
Drives that reached the opponent’s 20, per game.
EPA per playFree
Average expected points added per offensive play, overall and split by pass and rush.

Defenses and schedule

Points allowedFree
PPR points a defense allows per game to each position.
Schedule easeFree
Percentile rank (0–100) of points allowed to the position, shrunk toward league average early in the season. 100 is the softest matchup.
Matchup easeFree
Schedule ease blended with EPA allowed per dropback and per rush, weighted by position (for example 60% points, 40% pass EPA for receivers).
EPA allowedFree
Expected points added per dropback and per designed run that a defense gives up. Higher means easier.
Rest-of-season and playoff easeSeason Pass
Average schedule ease over a player’s remaining regular-season games, and over fantasy playoff Weeks 15 to 17.
