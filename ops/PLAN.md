# Fieldwren business plan (v1, 2026-10-09)

## One line
Advanced fantasy football analytics that update themselves every night, free to browse, $9 to unlock the full season.

## Customer
Season-long fantasy managers (redraft and dynasty) who already read usage stats and want the next level:
expected points, efficiency, team environment and playoff schedules, in one calm, fast place.
They pay for FantasyPros, PFF+ or 4for4 ($20-$40+/yr), so $9 for a season is an easy yes if the data is good.

## Why this can win
- It is a living product: the data rebuilds itself twice a day and the nightly agent ships improvements, so it
  cannot be copied with a one-shot AI prompt.
- Advanced metrics most free sites do not show together: xFP/FPOE, opportunity quality, efficiency percentiles,
  PROE/pace, blended matchup ease, playoff schedule, a composite score that explains itself.
- Spreadsheet feed for Excel/Sheets power users (our owner's world).

## Revenue model
- NFL Season Pass $9 one-time per season (Lemon Squeezy, merchant of record, license keys). Net ~$8.05.
- Next: Fantasy Premier League Season Pass (Nov launch, runs to May), then NFL draft kit + dynasty (Jul-Aug).
- Later options (decide with data, Skeptic review first): weekly price test, all-sports annual pass, email sponsorship.

## Distribution (in order of expected impact, first 30 days)
1. Charlie shares once in his own fantasy leagues and on his own X/LinkedIn (launch kit in marketing/launch/).
2. Daily data-driven posts on X and Bluesky from the brand account (agent-written, Action-posted).
3. Shareable player cards and "?player=" deep links; risers/fallers that people screenshot.
4. SEO: glossary + one static page per notable player and team (unique data each), sitemap, Search Console.
5. Weekly "Tuesday risers" email once the domain + Resend are set up.

## Costs
$0/month on free tiers (Convex, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Actions, Lemon Squeezy has no monthly fee).
Domain ~$10/yr. X posting ~$0.015 per text post from a $20 starter credit with a hard cap.

## Honest expectations
Month 1: $0-$100 likely; first sales depend mostly on Charlie's one-time share and fantasy playoff timing (wk 15-17
are mid-December, so playoff-schedule tools peak late Nov-Dec). Months 2-6: FPL + SEO compound.

## Kill / pivot criteria (reviewed Sundays by the Skeptic)
- 0 sales by Dec 1 with >1,000 visits: pricing/paywall problem -> test what is free vs Pro.
- <300 visits by Dec 1: distribution problem -> shift effort from features to content and shareables.
- Any payment-provider policy issue: switch provider (Gumroad fallback) the same week.
