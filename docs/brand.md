# Brand: The Daily Token

Owner decision 2026-10-09. A "token" is the word-piece an AI reads and writes, and it's also a coin. The channel hands
you one AI story at a time.

- Name: **The Daily Token**. Handles: @thedailytoken (fallbacks: @thedailytoken.ai, @dailytokennews).
- Tagline / bio: "AI news, animated. 3 stories a day."
- Hashtag on every post: #thedailytoken.
- Colors (same as the videos): lime `#c4ff3a` (accent), yellow `#ffe14a` (highlight), deep navy `#0c0a24` and
  near-black `#03020a` (backgrounds), off-white `#eef1f7` (robot).
- Mascot: the illustration kit's robot (white rounded head, dark visor, lime eyes, antenna with a lime tip).

## Logo prompt (profile picture, for an image model)

> Design a social media profile logo for an AI news channel called "The Daily Token". Concept: a glowing token coin
> with a friendly robot face on it. Flat vector illustration in a modern explainer-animation style: bold simple
> shapes, 2-3 tones per shape, one soft shadow, no photorealism, no 3D render.
>
> Subject, centered and front-facing: a round coin seen straight on, with a thick lime-green rim (#C4FF3A) and small
> notches around the edge like a real coin. On the face of the coin is a cute, confident robot head: rounded off-white
> head (#EEF1F7), a dark navy visor screen (#141A33) with two glowing lime-green rectangular eyes, slightly squinted in
> a friendly, curious expression, and a short antenna whose lime tip pokes just past the top of the rim. Add a tiny
> yellow (#FFE14A) sparkle on the rim to suggest "fresh / new".
>
> Background: solid deep navy (#0C0A24). It must read clearly as a tiny circular profile picture (110 px), so use bold
> shapes and no fine detail. No text, letters or numbers anywhere in the image. No real logos, no real people, no
> currency symbols. Square 1:1, high resolution, with generous padding so it survives a circular crop.

Ask for 4 variations and pick the one that still reads at thumbnail size.

## Wordmark prompt (banners and video end cards)

Montserrat Black from Google Fonts).
